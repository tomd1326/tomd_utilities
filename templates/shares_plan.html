  1. Keep the reserve in cash savings, with no more than the protected limit at any one bank (see Key Facts).
  2. Fill the Stocks & Shares ISA first: £20,000 before 5 April and another £20,000 from 6 April. Hold the rest in a general account until it can be moved in.
  3. Put the larger part into low-cost funds that hold the whole market, and use the smaller part for picks.
  4. Decide before buying how much of a fall in any one pick you will accept, and how often you will review (monthly is enough).
  5. Do not borrow to invest, and stay out of spread betting and CFDs.
